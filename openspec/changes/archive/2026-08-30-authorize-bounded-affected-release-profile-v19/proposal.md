## Why

Published investigation v19 закрыл design gaps hosted setup-node origin и
observed context-sensitive activation graph, но executable v19 остаётся
запрещённым без отдельной clean, reviewed и remotely published authorization.

## What Changes

- Зафиксировать exact six-field authorization object и единственный bounded
  implementation v19 successor с production ceiling `500`/implementation
  maximum `499` LOC.
- Зафиксировать exact dependency/block graph и sole two-field authorization
  reference будущей implementation.
- Перенести в successor обязательные token-specific hosted
  `RUNNER_TOOL_CACHE` witnesses, independently materialized activation rows,
  full bidirectional catalog equality и external dynamic reachable witness.
- Сохранить published v18 proof inventory `35/30/48`, оба immutable digest,
  total ownership/migration, accumulated affected floor и original-RED
  chronology без переиздания inventory.
- Оставить authorization docs-only, dormant и non-authoritative; implementation
  и certification surfaces не создавать.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- none.

### Modified Capabilities

- `changerail-release-ci`: добавить exact v19 authorization boundary для sole
  clean implementation и сохранить hosted/activation proof floor.

## Impact

Change затрагивает только board/OpenSpec documentation, synchronized
`openspec/specs/changerail-release-ci/spec.md` и archive metadata. Production,
tests, CI/runtime behavior и publication authority не изменяются; terminal v18
payload/evidence остаются forensic-only.
