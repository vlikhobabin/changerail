## Why

Published investigation v20 закрыл design gaps process ordering, hosted
ancestor containment/CI derivation и activation row/worklist connectivity, но
executable v20 остаётся запрещённым без отдельной clean, reviewed и remotely
published authorization.

## What Changes

- Зафиксировать exact six-field authorization object и единственный bounded
  implementation v20 successor с production ceiling `500`/implementation
  maximum `499` LOC.
- Зафиксировать exact dependency/block graph, authorization-publishing base и
  sole two-field authorization reference будущей implementation.
- Перенести в successor process-free Phase-A aggregate barrier, bounded Phase-B
  probes и externally observed zero-event negative neighbors.
- Перенести component-safe independently CI-derived `node`/`npm`/`npx` targets
  и единую source-derived activation worklist, row projection и exact-row
  dynamic equality.
- Сохранить published v18 proof inventory `35/30/48`, оба immutable digest,
  total ownership/migration, accumulated affected floor и original-RED
  chronology без переиздания inventory.
- Оставить authorization docs-only, dormant и non-authoritative;
  implementation и certification surfaces не создавать.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- none.

### Modified Capabilities

## Impact

Change затрагивает только board/OpenSpec documentation, synchronized
`openspec/specs/changerail-release-ci/spec.md` и archive metadata. Production,
tests, CI/runtime behavior и publication authority не изменяются; terminal v19
payload/evidence остаются forensic-only.
